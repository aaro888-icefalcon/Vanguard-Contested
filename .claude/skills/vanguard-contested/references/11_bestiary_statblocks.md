# 11 · BESTIARY & THREAT STAT BLOCKS (M&M 3e)
*The threat palette of file 05, statted for play. Every block is **M&M 3e RAW** and keyed to a rung via the conversion spec (file 01). PC-band blocks (Bruiser–Breaker) are **audited against PL caps**; boss-band blocks (Warlord+) **deliberately exceed PL 8–9 caps** and are marked — they are statted to be fled, not dueled. Damage save DC = 15 + Damage rank; Affliction DC = 10 + rank.*

> **The deadliness rule (file 05):** tune the encounter to the **region band**, not the PC's confidence. In a Guarded redoubt a Breaker is near-dominant; at an Embattled edge or in No-Man's-Land, Warlord+ threats are present and the PC can die. The blocks below are sorted by what belongs where.

---

## HOW TO USE THESE BLOCKS

- **Minions** drop on a single failed Toughness save (RAW minion rule: any failure takes them out; they roll/are-rolled one degree worse). Run them in numbers; the **mass** is the threat, not the individual. Feral and rank-and-file go here.
- **Troops** are full creatures meant to be fielded 3–6 at a time. A PL 8–9 PC can beat one; a band wears the PC down (attrition is the campaign's real killer). Reaver raiders, Empire troopers.
- **Named / bosses** are individuals. A Heavy-elite is the *upper bound of a fair fight*; **Warlord+ is above PL — flee.** Their dossiers live in file 12.
- **Scaling a block:** shift one rung ≈ ±1–2 PL ≈ ±2 to attack/effect/defenses and ±1–2 to the signature power rank. Add minions to raise lethality without raising tier.
- **Honest resolution:** roll attacks and saves with code, show them, apply degrees exactly (bruised → dazed → staggered → incapacitated), and read incapacitation per the lethality mod (death/maiming/capture, harsh default).

## FERAL — the ambient constant

### Feral Beast *(Bruiser, PL 5 · MINION)* — a dog, deer, rat-thing, gull-thing turned monster
- **Abilities:** STR 4 · STA 3 · AGL 3 · DEX 0 · FGT 4 · INT −4 · AWE 1 · PRE −2
- **Defenses:** Dodge 6 · Parry 5 · Fort 5 · Will 2 · **Toughness 4**
- **Offense:** Close +5, **Damage 5** (claws/teeth, DC 20)
- **Skills:** Perception +6, Stealth +6 (small) / +0 (large), Athletics +8
- **Senses/Move:** Scent (Acute smell), low-light; Speed 2 (or Swimming 3 for marine/river feral)
- **Advantages:** Improved Critical (bite), Move-by Action
- **Tactics:** packs; harries, drags down the slow, breaks contact when hurt. *Marine/river variants own the water — Swimming, ambush from below.*
- **Cap audit (PL5, 2×=10):** Atk 5 + Dmg 5 = 10 ✓ · Dodge 6 + Tough 4 = 10 ✓ · Parry 5 + Tough 4 = 9 ✓

### Sapient Pack-Leader *(Heavy, PL 7 · TROOP/named)* — the cunning edge of the swarm
- **Abilities:** STR 5 · STA 5 · AGL 4 · DEX 1 · FGT 7 · INT −1 (tactically cunning) · AWE 3 · PRE 0
- **Defenses:** Dodge 9 · Parry 8 · Fort 7 · Will 5 · **Toughness 5**
- **Offense:** Close +8, **Damage 6** (DC 21); Improved Grab (drag-down)
- **Skills:** Perception +9, Stealth +8, Athletics +9, Intimidation +6
- **Senses/Move:** Acute scent + tracking, Danger Sense; Speed 3, Leaping 2
- **Advantages:** Improved Initiative, Set-up (feeds flanks to the pack), Improved Critical, Evasion
- **Tactics:** ambushes, drives prey into the pack, spends minions freely, **flees from anything bigger** (file 08 monster behavior 9).
- **Cap audit (PL7, 2×=14):** Atk 8 + Dmg 6 = 14 ✓ · Dodge 9 + Tough 5 = 14 ✓ · Parry 8 + Tough 5 = 13 ✓

**Swarm handling (the mass, no will):** when the threat is *numbers*, don't roll every beast — run a **wave**: a forced Toughness or Dodge save each round at a DC set by the region's lethality (≈ DC 15 + lethality-step), failure = injury + a Supply/positioning cost, and the wave keeps coming until the PC breaks contact or finds a choke. The swarm doesn't duel; it buries. Coasts and rivers are death because the wave never ends.

## HOSTILE EMPOWERED HUMANS — the wild card *(templates; swap the power)*
The lost zones are full of powered people with no oversight. Build one by taking a frame below and **rolling the manifested power on file 08 (d20)**, slotting its rank at the frame's tier. Example signature powers drawn from the foundation pass are noted.

## COORDINATED ORDINARY FORCE — *"you sleep"*
Not a creature — a **principle**: numbers and sustained heavy fire wear down even a Siege over time. The PC has to eat, rest, and heal; a disciplined force doesn't have to win a fight, only deny those.

### Militia / Soldier Squad *(PL 4 · MINIONS, 4–10)*
- **Abilities:** STR 1 · STA 2 · AGL 1 · DEX 2 · FGT 3 · INT 0 · AWE 1 · PRE 0
- **Defenses:** Dodge 4 · Parry 4 · Fort 4 · Will 3 · **Toughness 4** (STA 2 + cover/vest 2)
- **Offense:** salvaged rifle Ranged +3, **Damage 5** (DC 20); concentrated fire = a **Team Attack** (many minions stack into a single high attack roll — this, not the +3, is how a squad hurts a Breaker)
- **Tactics:** cover, crossfire, suppress, call for the heavy thing. They lose the fight and win the night.
- **Cap audit (PL4, 2×=8):** Atk 3 + Dmg 5 = 8 ✓ · Dodge 4 + Tough 4 = 8 ✓ · Parry 4 + Tough 4 = 8 ✓ *(the lethal attack comes from massed Team Attacks, not the individual bonus)*
- **Attrition rule:** against a higher-tier PC, don't fight fair — **siege, starve, wait, and mass fire.** A clock toward exhaustion (Supply, Fatigue) is deadlier than any single roll.

## MONSTER-LORDS — campaign-scale *(Titan–Horror · BOSS — far above PL; not an encounter)*

### Monster-Lord Frame *(kaiju-class sapient, the 1,000× tail)*
*A force of nature with a will. **It is a setting event, not a fight.** The interaction is evacuation, evasion, denial, and survival — never a duel.*
- **Scale:** Toughness 16–20+, **Impervious Toughness 14+** (PC-band damage simply does not register), area attacks (Burst/Line Damage 12+), Movement (Burrowing/Swimming/Flight, vast Speed), Senses across miles.
- **Running it:** it has **goals and a path** (feed, nest, migrate, claim ground — file 08 monster behavior). The PC's rolls are to *get out of the way*, save who they can, and pay the Supply/injury cost of the ground it takes. Damaging it is a campaign arc (a relic, a faction's combined weight, a rift, an exploit), not an action.
- **Canon exemplar:** **the Drowning Crown** (Horror-tier, marine — file 12) closed the North Atlantic and is why the sea off New England is death. A rift-touched kaiju grinding inland is a moving deadline, not a boss to corner.

## THE INHUMAN, BOTH SIDES *(sorted by function — revision §10 / files 16–18)*
The non-human threats split by **how they behave**. *Willful* ones (intent — fight, ally, be ruled by) are statted as foes/faction actors below; *force-of-nature* ones (the world-breakers) run as hazards, waves, and clocks — never duels.

### WILLFUL — fightable foes & faction actors

**Sapient Monster-Lord Scion** *(Breaker, PL 9 · NAMED, fightable)* — a young or lesser district-beast with a will; the fightable cousin of the Titan+ queen above.
- **Abilities:** STR 8 · STA 8 · AGL 2 · DEX 0 · FGT 7 · INT −2 (cunning) · AWE 3 · PRE 2
- **Defenses:** Dodge 4 · Parry 7 · Fort 9 · Will 6 · **Toughness 11** (STA 8 + hide 3), **Impervious 7**
- **Offense:** Close +7, **Damage 11** (rending, DC 26); Improved Grab/Hold
- **Powers/senses:** Regeneration 4 · Acute scent + tracking · Speed 3, Leaping 2 · leads feral (Set-up)
- **Neglected:** **Will** (6) and **Dodge** (4) — slow; soft to a mind/affliction effect and to ranged/area. **Tactics:** territorial, cunning, herds feral at you; a Breaker can take it but it can dumpster you hurt.
- **Cap audit (PL9, 2×=18):** Atk 7 + Dmg 11 = 18 ✓ · Parry 7 + Tough 11 = 18 ✓ · Dodge 4 + Tough 11 = 15 ✓

**Spirit / Transcendent-Touched Entity** *(Breaker, PL 9 · NAMED, uncanny)* — a lesser rift/spirit power; *full transcendents are above-PL queens.*
- **Abilities:** STR 0 · STA 2 · AGL 4 · DEX 4 · FGT 4 · INT 2 · AWE 6 · PRE 4
- **Defenses:** Dodge 9 · Parry 7 · Fort 6 · Will 9 · **Toughness 4** (Insubstantial 2 — partial; physical attacks at −half unless a counter-descriptor)
- **Offense:** **Perception-range Affliction 9** (Will; dazed → compelled → controlled, DC 19); descriptor: spirit/mind
- **Neglected:** **Toughness/Fort** — physically frail; ground it (anti-spirit descriptor, a relic, file 14 Lodestone) and hit it hard. **Tactics:** never melees; maddens, compels, turns your allies; flees the body, haunts the place.
- **Cap audit (PL9):** perception Affliction rank 9 ≤ PL ✓ · Dodge 9 + Tough 4 = 13 ✓ · Parry 7 + Tough 4 = 11 ✓

**Rift-Crosser Champion** *(Heavy–Breaker, PL 8–9 · NAMED)* — an elite of one of the 18 crosser nations (file 04/13), peelable sub-faction. Use the **Powered Human Heavy/Breaker frame** + one **alien capability** (a crosser-tech weapon, an unusual descriptor, or a bloc power) and a culture-bound temperament. Cap as that frame.

**Mastered Transformed-Powered** *(Breaker, PL 9 · NAMED)* — a human the Pulse remade who **kept their mind.** Use the **Powered Human Breaker frame** (file 11) with a **body-horror mutation** signature (extra reach/limbs, a toxin, regeneration, a carapace = Impervious). They sit on the roster as rivals or allies (file 16); the *lost* version is below.

**Lesser Sapient Kaiju** *(Siege, PL 10–11 · ABOVE PC — a rook)* — a kaiju you can *only* beat with terrain, allies, surprise, or a blind-spot. Toughness 13 / **Impervious 9**, area attack (Burst Damage 11), vast Speed; a willful queen-in-waiting that can **lead a faction** (thralls, a cult — file 18). Statted above PC caps on purpose.

### FORCE-OF-NATURE — survival & disaster frames (tier-ignoring; run as hazards/waves/clocks)

- **Feral Wave** *(the swarm, no will).* Don't roll each beast — run a **wave**: one forced Toughness/Dodge save per round at **DC 15 + the locale's effective-lethality steps** (file 17 §2); failure = injury + a Supply/position cost; it keeps coming until the PC breaks contact or finds a choke. Marine/river waves never end — coasts are death.
- **Rampaging Kaiju (as weather).** Not a statblock — a **moving-deadline clock** (4–6): each scene it advances along its path (feed/nest/migrate). The PC's rolls are to **evade, evacuate, and deny**, paying the Supply/injury cost of ground it takes. Ignores tier; cornering it is a death scene.
- **The Unmaking Creep.** A **growing-zone clock** (file 14 Greyfield): a die-back/wrong zone widening each interval, instruments failing at its edge. The answer is flee or contain (a relic, a rift-seal arc), never fight. Survival-horror register.
- **Lost Transformed-Powered (feral horror).** A former person consumed by the change — run for **dread**, not stats: limited information, a thing that stalks the camp and can't be reasoned with. If forced to a frame, a Heavy–Breaker with a mutation and **no Will to appeal to**; killing it is a grim mercy and may scar whoever knew them (Legacy, file 16).
