# 05 · BESTIARY & NAMED POWERS
*What can kill a PC, by tier, plus the individuals who matter at the world scale. All figures fictional; named powers carry voice.*

## Threat palette — what can kill you

| Threat | Typical tier | Lethal to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feral swarm** | 1–5× each, vast numbers | any PC, by mass | won't duel you; buries you. Marine swarms own the coasts and the sea. The ambient constant. |
| **Sapient feral / pack-leaders** | Bruiser–Heavy | Bruiser–Breaker | cunning, coordinated animals; the dangerous edge of the swarm. |
| **Reaver war-band** | members Heavy; chief Heavy–Warlord | Breaker | the Mid-Atlantic's daily threat; a chief outclasses a starting PC. |
| **Empire vanguard cohort** | troops Bruiser–Heavy; officers Warlord | Breaker–Siege | disciplined, coordinated — the difference between a mob and an army. |
| **Empire champion** | Warlord–Crown | Siege and below | the Empire fields powers above the PC band; treat as boss-tier. |
| **Monster-lord (kaiju-class)** | Titan–Horror (the 1,000× sapient tail) | everything local | a rare beast that breaks a district; a campaign event, not an encounter. |
| **Hostile empowered human / warlord** | Bruiser–Titan | depends | other powered people, some far above you, with no oversight in the lost zones. |
| **Coordinated ordinary force** | n/a | any tier, over time | you sleep; numbers and sustained heavy weapons wear down even a Siege. |

**Deadliness rule:** in a Guarded redoubt a Breaker is near-dominant; at an Embattled edge or in No-Man's-Land, Warlord+ threats are present and a PC can die. Tune encounters to the region's danger band, not the PC's confidence.

## Named powers (region-significant roster)

*Starter roster — expand freely. Tier in colloquial rung; all fictional.*

### The Empire
- **The Iron Regent** (Crown, ~300×) — the Empire's field commander in the West, charged with turning the heartland's weight toward the redoubts once consolidation completes. Cold, procedural, treats conquest as administration. *Voice: clipped, bureaucratic, never raises it — "The line moves on schedule. Sentiment is not a variable."*
- **Vurn of the Seventh Spear** (Warlord) — vanguard officer, the human redoubts' most likely first named Empire foe.

### The Reavers (New England)
- **Hesk Gravewater** (Warlord) — the closest thing the Reavers have to a paramount chief, holding the Connecticut approaches. Charismatic, capricious, genuinely believes the Reavers are also refugees owed land. *Voice: warm then sudden, fond of bleak proverbs — "Everyone's running from something, friend. We just stopped."*
- **The Knot-Sisters** (two Heavies, paired) — raid-leaders who can be peeled away; tired of dying for nothing.

### The redoubt & its allies
- **Marshal of the Interior Line** (unpowered, or a Heavy) — the human authority running the NA redoubt's defense and the conscription system; the PCs' likely commander and the face of the autonomy-vs-duty tension.
- **Sael-of-the-Open-Hand** (Compact, Titan) — the Interior Compact's envoy and war-leader, the ally who makes the redoubt viable. Patient, curious about humans, grieving a lost homeland. *Voice: formal, gentle, asks more than answers — "Show me how you decide who eats. Then I will know who you are."*
- **First Voice of the Concord** (Concord, Crown) — the wavering elite ally's representative; will not commit while humanity looks doomed. Winning them over is the campaign's great diplomatic prize. *Voice: precise, sorrowful, maddeningly reasonable — "We have buried one world. We are careful which graves we dig beside."*

### Humanity, in the wild
- **The Breaker of Pittsburgh** (Siege) — an empowered strongman who carved a fief from a corridor city; protector to his people, tyrant to the rest. The moral-gray human power the PCs will measure themselves against.
- **Doc** (Heavy, regeneration/field-medicine) — a wandering healer-protector of the evacuation roads; a model of the quiet, decent use of power. *(A natural recurring ally.)*

### Monster-lords (campaign-scale)
- **The Drowning Crown** (Horror-tier, marine) — a kaiju-class sapient that closed the North Atlantic shipping lanes; the reason the sea is death off New England. Not an encounter — a force of nature with a will.
