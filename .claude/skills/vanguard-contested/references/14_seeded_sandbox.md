## LOCAL FACTIONS *(file 04 origin/drive + file 09 tags — emergent, Tier 1–3)*

### The Elevator — *Tier 1, Weak*
*[origin d8=7 industrial remnant · drive d6=2 order/control · tags: Granary + Armed to the Teeth]*
A grain-elevator and its silos in the Embattled farm-belt, held by the workforce and whoever picked up the guns. It **feeds its hinterland and rules it by the ration**, and it's armed above its weight — but it's small, fixed, and a prize everyone covets. *Asset: food. Weakness: can't project past the silo walls.* **Clock — "impose the tithe / hold the harvest": 6.** *Hook:* the Authority wants its grain (Mission 3); the Drift wants to strip it; the Elevator wants protection it can't pay for.

### The Drift — *Tier 3, Strong*
*[origin d8=4 grown enclave · drive d6=3 predation · tags: Industrialists + Nomadic]*
A roving salvage-works — a mobile column of mechanics, muscle, and stripped trucks that **cannibalizes infrastructure and moves on**, leaving plants and crossings picked clean. They are who stripped **Leg 4's river crossing** (file 10). Strong, mobile, predatory; can't be besieged because they never stop. *Asset: mobility + the only working heavy salvage gear on the frontier. Weakness: makes enemies everywhere, sustains nothing.* **Clock — "strip the corridor and roll west": 4.** *Hook:* they have what Mira Larkin needs to keep a vehicle running — and they take, they don't trade.

### The Ward — *Tier 1, Weak*
*[origin d8=7 industrial remnant · drive d6=2 order/control · tags: Medical Lifeline + Armed to the Teeth]*
A clinic or pharma-distribution site in the corridor, held by its medical staff and an armed guard, **rationing the last real medicine for miles** and trying to impose a fragile order around it. Indispensable, courted by all, and bled the instant its drugs are needed elsewhere. A natural home for **Oziel Voss** (file 12) — and the kind of place that hardens into a Medical-Lifeline faction. *Asset: medicine. Weakness: everyone needs it, no one can defend it.* **Clock — "ration the last stock before it's taken": 4.** *Hook:* the PC will need what the Ward has; so will the Reavers, the cults, and the Indenture.

### The Indenture — *Tier 3, Weak · DOOMED*
*[origin d8=8 mercenary company · drive d6=2 order/control · tags: Granary + Doomed]*
A detached PMC that seized a food store and a walled holding — **Leg 3's stronghold that seems too quiet** (file 10). It offers rest, trade, and a clean welcome, and behind it is a press for bodies: the Indenture is **running out of something vital** (fuel, legitimacy, time) and quietly conscripting the able to outlast it. *Asset: a strongpoint + food. Weakness: a clock is killing it, and it knows.* **Clock — "fill the ranks before the fuel runs dry": 4.** *Hook:* the welcome is real until you try to leave; desperate factions make the worst deals and the worst enemies.

## MISSION SEEDS *(file 09 — job · catch · stakes · patron; wired to the sandbox)*

1. **Cut the Tower** — *[remove a leader · the truth is worse than the job · territory · a local militia]* A corridor militia hires the PC to remove the **Leg-1 tower strongman** (file 10) and free the chokepoint. The truth: he's the only thing keeping a Reaver band off the route — kill him and the Tide rolls in.
2. **The Grinding Hour** — *[buy time for someone else · a monster-lord holds the ground · intel/a map · no patron]* The PC stumbles into a fleeing column whose only path runs past a **rift-touched kaiju** grinding inland. No one sent you; buying the column an hour costs you, and the map they carry is the only reason to.
3. **The Last Silo** — *[secure a resource · a rival wants it too · food · a dying official]* A dying Authority official sends the PC to secure **the Elevator's** grain before **the Drift** strips it. Two clocks, one harvest.
4. **Saltbroke's Pen** — *[rescue captives · a rival wants it too · a safe place · a local militia]* Rescue captives from **Varn Saltbroke's** refugee-mass (file 13) — but the Drift (or another band) wants those bodies too. The reward is a safe place, if anyone's left to give it.
5. **The Quarry Den** — *[hunt a specific threat · the swarm is in the way · a working vehicle · a dying official]* Clear the feral lair pressing on **Marrow Creek's Quarry** (file 10); the feral wave (file 11) is between you and it. Reward: a running vehicle — likely the one **Mira Larkin** keeps alive.
6. **Too Late at the Plant** — *[scout a zone · it's already too late, pivot · medicine · the Redoubt Authority]* The Authority sends the PC to scout the **water-plant gate** (Leg 5) ahead of a convoy — and it's already fallen or changed. The job pivots; medicine is the pay, if the PC brings back the truth fast enough.
7. **The Crosser's Knife** — *[remove a leader · you're a pawn · territory · a crosser]* A crosser (perhaps **Nadia Mwangi**, file 12, or a peelable Reaver) asks the PC to remove a rival chief — and the PC is a piece in a sub-faction power play whose real prize is ground the crosser will hold.
8. **Hold the Causeway** — *[buy time for someone else · the swarm is in the way · a working vehicle · the Interior Compact]* **Sael's Compact** (file 13) asks the PC to hold a crossing while their people withdraw, with the swarm running the water between. Earn the Compact's regard — and a vehicle — at the cost of standing where the wave comes.

## CULTS *(file 09 — the corridor's warring creeds; an engine-rolled mirror)*

### The Open Hand *(the radio creed)* — *"the crossers are saviors"*
*[belief d12=4 crossers are saviors · leader d10=9 a voice on the radio · practice d10=7 marking the faithful · bites d10=7 spreads a lie that gets people killed]*
A movement spread by **a voice on the radio** — one of the rare shielded transmitters, looping a gospel that the crossers came to *save*, not conquer, and that the marked faithful will be spared. Followers wear the mark and walk toward the rifts and the Compact. The lie that kills: it tells people to **lower their guard before the wrong crossers** (Reavers, Empire scouts) and lay down arms that would have saved them. *Resonates with the looping signal rumor (file 08 · word from the road).*

### The Quiet Child *(the rift creed)* — *"the crossers are demons"*
*[belief d12=5 crossers are demons · leader d10=7 a 'miracle' child · practice d10=10 rites at rift-sites · bites d10=7 spreads a lie that gets people killed]*
The mirror cult: a **"miracle" child** at its center, whose handlers preach that the crossers are demons and the rifts are wounds to be sealed with rites — and blood. They gather **at rift-sites** (file 14 below), and the lie that kills is purity: they name survivors "tainted" — the empowered, crosser-kin, the marked of the Open Hand — and get them killed. **The two cults are at war**, and the corridor burns in the gap between them. *A PC who is empowered, or kind to crossers, is a target for the Quiet Child.*

## RIFT SITES & ANOMALIES *(file 09 — what · hazard · sign)*

### The Greyfield — *a die-back zone*
*[what d12=6 die-back zone · hazard d10=9 spreads slowly, a clock · sign d8=6 the rare working instrument fails wrongly]*
A patch where **nothing lives and nothing will** — grass to grey ash, animals fled, the few shielded instruments going wrong at its edge. It is **growing on a clock**, eating road and farmland by the week. An Unmaking-adjacent dread made local: a creeping deadline no one can fight, only flee or watch. *Set a slow clock; let it widen a leg of the route over the campaign.*

### The Open Door — *a crosser-arrival site*
*[what d12=10 crosser-arrival site · hazard d10=10 offers something valuable for a price · sign d8=6 instruments fail wrongly]*
A rift **still disgorging** — crossers (and worse) still come through here. It **offers value for a price**: salvage, a crosser contact, even passage, to anyone willing to deal at the threshold. The **Quiet Child** does its rites here; the **Open Hand** pilgrimages toward it. A place to gain a crosser ally, a relic, or a knife in the dark — and the cults fight over the ground.

### The Green Hollow — *a fertile/healing anomaly (a rare boon, with a shadow)*
*[what d12=9 fertile/healing anomaly · hazard d10=5 plain physical danger · sign d8=4 survivors speak of "the quiet that eats"]*
A rare **good** anomaly: a hollow where wounds knit faster, water runs clean, and food grows where it shouldn't — a place to heal lingering injuries and resupply. But the way in is **physically lethal** (a flooded gorge, a collapsing approach, the swarm at its mouth), and survivors whisper of **"the quiet that eats"** at its heart — an Unmaking sign under the gift. A boon worth the danger, and a thread worth pulling carefully.

## RELICS *(file 09 — every relic a gift with a price)*

1. **The Strider** — *[a piece of crosser tech · gift: mobility, it runs · catch: anomalous/unpredictable]* A crosser conveyance that still moves in a dead-grid world — priceless, and unreliable: it runs until it doesn't, goes where it shouldn't, draws eyes. **Mira Larkin** (file 12) is the one who can coax it; the Drift would take it.
2. **The Mercy-Engine** — *[a piece of crosser tech · gift: healing/medicine · catch: a moral cost]* A crosser medical device that heals true — but every use **takes something** (a finite crosser substance the Compact grieves, or vitality drawn from another). A gift that asks who you'll spend it on. Natural in the hands of **Oziel Voss** or **Doc**.
3. **The Lodestone** — *[an empowered's lost focus · gift: suppresses or amplifies powers · catch: anomalous/unpredictable]* A dead empowered's focus-object that **damps or boosts powers** in its field — unpredictably which. A prize for any powered faction, a leveler against a Breaker, a wild card in a fight.
4. **The Crown-Shard** — *[a transcendent relic · gift: suppresses or amplifies powers · catch: it draws monsters]* A shard of something transcendent that **amplifies (or stills) powers** — and **draws feral and worse** to it for miles. Carrying it is a moving siege; it ties to the deep horizon, and the Quiet Child would kill to seal it or wield it.
