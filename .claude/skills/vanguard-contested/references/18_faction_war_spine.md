# 18 · THE FACTION-WAR SPINE (Authority Strata · Standing & Rank · Dynamic Board)
*The full subsystem for faction-vs-faction play: how the human authority strata, warlords, crossers, and willful monster-powers wage war across a **dynamically-generated board**, how the PC's **per-faction Standing** and **Rank** work, and how the PC tips engagements. Builds on file 04 (the faction list), files 13/14 (the frontier dossiers and seeded factions), and files 16/17 (the roster and the Director). Roll the war; show the perceivable clocks; never soften an outcome.*

> **Diplomacy is sub-faction** (file 04): you turn a war-band, a chief, a house, a precinct — not a whole divided bloc. Unitary powers (Empire, Concord, Compact) treat as wholes.

---

## 1 · THE DYNAMIC BOARD (continuity across a moving game)
There is no fixed contested city — the **board is wherever the PC is** (generated by the Director, file 17 §2). What makes it a *war* rather than scattered fights is **persistence**:
- **Factions, roster cards, Standing, and clocks carry between locales.** Leave a town and the faction you wronged there remembers, sends word down its network (§3), and may follow.
- **The six world clocks** (file 04) are the macro-war; **local war-fronts** (§2) are the micro-war on the current board. The Director ties a local front to a world clock where it makes sense (a Reaver front ticks the Reaver Tide).
- **The coastal-heat gradient** (file 17 §2) decides how hot the local war runs: nearer the coast / later in the year, fronts move faster and crueler.

---

## 2 · WAR-FRONTS & WAR-CLOCKS (how a war runs, and how the PC tips it)
A **front** is a contested thing between two factions: territory, a resource (granary, fuel, water, meds, a relic), a chokepoint, a population, or a person. Give each live front a **war-clock** (4–8 segments) toward one side's victory.

**Advancing a front (offscreen, rolled each interval):** roll the faction's move (file 08 Faction Move d20) weighted by relative Tier/Hold and assets; a win ticks the clock toward that side. Show the player only the fronts they can perceive.

**The PC tips it (this is the play):**
- Win a **set-piece battle** on the front (file 17 §5) → tick 1–2 toward your side.
- Take or deny the **prize** (raid the granary, blow the bridge, extract the person) → tick or freeze the clock.
- **Turn or break a powered/command** card (file 16) → drop the enemy's Strength a step.
- **Peel a sub-faction** (diplomacy, a shared enemy, tribute) → move a war-band off the board entirely.
- **Standing & Rank** (below) buy you troops, intel, and orders that move clocks at scale.

**When a front fills:** the loser is **absorbed / scattered / mutated** (file 04), the map redraws, Standing shifts across the network (§3), and new fronts open. The board should look different after every war-clock resolves.

---

## 3 · PER-FACTION STANDING (no global meter; it spreads by network)
Standing is tracked **per faction** on the canon relationship track (file 04): **−3 war · −2 hostile · −1 friction · 0 neutral · +1 cooperative · +2 allied · +3 sworn.** Each faction the PC touches carries its own value in `vanguard-state.md`. There is **no single reputation number** — you can be **+3 sworn** to the Authority and **−3 at war** with a warlord at once.

**Network spread (the only "global" effect):** word travels along ties. When Standing moves with one faction, it **bleeds at reduced strength** to that faction's allies (same direction) and enemies (opposite): a Reaver band you wronged warns kin; the Authority's good word runs its own chain; the powered community and the road carry your name *locally*. Roll the spread on the oracle when it's unclear how far word has reached. The **Exposure clock** (SKILL.md) feeds this — a discharge can crater your Standing with the faction that just learned your nature.

## 4 · RANK (when you join — privileges and a leash)
**Standing is how a faction feels about you; Rank is your place inside it.** Joining needs sufficient Standing plus a joining beat, and grants a **Rank** with **privileges** (assets, supply, subordinates, intel, safe ground, the right to give orders that move war-clocks) and **obligations** (orders, conscription, accountability — a military faction can court-martial you; a warlord can spend you). Rank advances by service and can be **stripped or demoted**. Ladders are faction-typed:

| Faction type | Rank ladder |
|---|---|
| **Military / Authority** | conscript/recruit → trooper → sergeant → officer → command |
| **Warlord band** | hangabout → sworn → lieutenant → right hand |
| **Cult / movement** | seeker → marked → adept → voice |
| **Crosser sub-faction** | outsider → guest-friend → blood-bound *(varies by bloc)* |
| **Civic / enclave** | resident → trusted → council |

You can hold high Standing **without** joining (a trusted outside ally), or join low and climb. **Multiple memberships** are possible and dangerous — divided loyalties, conflicting orders, and the transition that ends careers: a patron faction you defy turns Rival/Nemesis (file 16 §4). Rank ties directly to the roster: your patron, your CO, your war-band chief are relationship cards.

## 5 · THE HUMAN AUTHORITY STRATA (the legitimacy gradient)
Society didn't fully fall; it **thickens westward**. These are distinct factions, layered, denser toward the redoubt — the third side of the war beyond warlords and crossers (all grounded in file 04).

| Stratum | Who | Hold | Strongest in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local / civic** | mayors, councils, supers, mutual-aid, congregations | weak, legitimate | everywhere, thin |
| **County / local law** | sheriffs, county forces, deputized posses | medium; de-facto gov in much of the land | the Embattled middle |
| **State** | governors + State/National Guard units | strong (interior states cohere fastest) | the forming redoubt |
| **National remnants** | federal continuity-of-government, NORTHCOM, FEMA | hardened but blind; nuclear = signal-only | deep interior installations |
| **Local militias** | preppers, county militias, armed civilians | variable — holdout backbone *or* warlord seed | rural, grassroots |
| **The Redoubt Authority** | the coalescing successor (Guard + interior states + Compact) | Tier 4, Strong (Marrow Creek garrison, file 13) | the Guarded west |

**The gradient in play:** East / No-Man's-Land = no law, might-makes-right. Middle / Embattled = fragile contested order; sheriffs, militias, warlords, and a flickering chain of command claim the same ground (the hottest board). West / Guarded = registration, conscription, ration law, military courts — power is a *regulated legal status*, and the Authority can deputize, draft, arrest, or try you. The lower strata **feed the Redoubt Authority** over the year (a county that cohered, a Guard unit that linked up) — a reconstruction front the PC can speed or break.

## 6 · NON-HUMAN & MONSTER-LED FACTIONS (the willful inhuman)
Per the function sort (revision plan §10), **willful** inhuman powers are faction actors here (the unfightable force-of-nature ones live in file 17 §6 / file 11):
- **Monster-lord domains** — a sapient kaiju-class beast with territory and **thralls** (a human/crosser cult feeding it — file 09 "harbors a real monster"). Run as a faction with a queen at its head (flee the queen; fight its servants).
- **Spirit / transcendent cults** — a claimed rift-domain (file 14) and the movement around it; a foot in the metaplot.
- **Rift-crosser nations & war-bands** — the 18 blocs and their champions (files 04/13), peelable sub-faction.
- **Monsters as assets** — a warlord with a leashed pack-leader, a band bound to something in the dark; the fightable willful threats (file 11) statted as faction pieces.
A monster-led faction has Tier/Hold, a front, a war-clock, and a Standing track like any other — you can war it, starve it, or (rarely, dangerously) treat with it.

## 7 · STATE TRACKING (`vanguard-state.md`)
Carry a **War Fronts** block: each live front = *factions · prize · war-clock (n/n) · which world clock it ticks · what the PC has done to it.* Carry the **Faction Standing & Rank** ledger: per-faction track + any membership Rank + obligations owed. Update both at scene end; redraw the board when a front resolves.
