# 03 · REGIONS
*Each region carries a danger band (from sim status), a lethality number (sim casualty rate), its crosser neighbors, and play hooks. The **North American redoubt frontier** is the default starting sandbox and gets the most detail.*

Danger bands: **Guarded** (redoubt) · **Embattled** (contested) · **Hostile-Held / No-Man's-Land** (overrun).

## North American redoubt frontier (default play area)

### NA Interior North — Guarded · ~8% *(safest large zone on Earth)*
The redoubt's heart, anchored by the **Interior Compact** (Bloc 3) ally. Reconstituted towns under emergency authority, ration economy, patchwork power returning, mixed human-Compact settlements. **Sites:** the relief-and-registration hubs, a Compact enclave-town, rebuilt rail spurs, a forward muster-camp facing the corridor. **Encounters:** registration/conscription friction, refugee intake, feral incursions from the lakes, Compact diplomacy, supply banditry. *Where PCs base.*

### NA Interior South / Mountain West / Canada Core — Guarded · ~10–11%
The deeper redoubt: food country, mountain refuges, the northern line. Quieter, scarcer, more self-reliant. **Encounters:** wilderness feral, refugee strain, hoarder-strongmen, the long supply road.

### Mid-Atlantic Corridor (DC–Baltimore–Philadelphia–NYC) — No-Man's-Land · ~18%
The contested belt being given up. New York largely lost as a unified city; governance relocated inland; holdout enclaves remain. **Sites:** the evacuation routes west, holdout-borough enclaves, dead hospitals and dark skyscrapers, Reaver raid-lines pressing from the north. **Encounters:** rearguard actions, extraction runs into holdouts, Reaver war-bands, scavenger crews, the desperate who stayed. *The frontier's lethal edge — and its richest adventure ground.*

### New England — No-Man's-Land · ~18% (and half-emptied)
The **Reaver** (Bloc 9) incursion zone: decentralized, high-per-capita warbands, no throne to treat with. **Encounters:** Reaver war-chiefs (Heavy–Warlord), raids, the occasional peelable Reaver individual (orientation is a member-average), lost survivors.

### NA Pacific Coast / Southeast — No-Man's-Land / Embattled · ~16–18%
Coastal, marine-monster-pressured, import-starved. **Encounters:** marine feral surges, famine-zone desperation, coastal corsair probes (Southeast).

### Mexico & Central America — Embattled · ~17%
A pressured contested zone feeding refugees north into the redoubt.

## Europe

### Northern Europe — Guarded · ~13%
The European redoubt, anchored by the trade-confederation (Bloc 11). **Encounters:** the held line, refugee intake from the south and east, Empire's western probes.

### Western Europe — Embattled · ~16% / Southern Europe–Med — No-Man's-Land · ~17% / Eastern Europe — No-Man's-Land · ~17%
Western Europe bleeds but holds in places; the Mediterranean takes Warrior-Kingdom pressure from the Middle East; Eastern Europe absorbs the Empire's western reach. **Encounters:** front-line war, Warrior-Kingdom raiders, Empire vanguard skirmishes, collapsing cities.

## Eurasia & Asia

### Central Asia–Siberia — No-Man's-Land · ~16% *(Empire heartland)* / Russia & Moscow — No-Man's-Land · ~16%
The **Empire's** seat and its great land vanguard's front. Hostile-held, depopulated, the gravitational center of the war. **Encounters:** Empire champions (Warlord–Crown), occupation, organized conquest — the deep hostile interior, for high-tier play only.

### Middle East — No-Man's-Land · ~18% *(Warrior-Kingdom, Bloc 5)*
A martial feudal crosser-kingdom's domain; a southern hostile arc.

### South Asia / India — Guarded · ~15% *(held but bloodied)*
The largest population anchor, held by the pilgrim-nation ally (Bloc 15) at heavy cost. A vast, crowded, embattled redoubt — a second great theatre of survival. **Encounters:** mass-scale defense, refugee oceans, ally diplomacy, monster surges along the coasts.

### East Asia / China — No-Man's-Land · ~16% *(squeezed)* / Japan & Korea — No-Man's-Land · ~19% *(encircled)*
East Asia is the hardest-pressed major theatre: the Empire from the north, the **Corsair Order** at sea, and the partly-defected **Pacific Houses** on the rim. Japan and Korea, island-dependent and encircled, take the worst of it. **Encounters:** three-way encirclement war, siege conditions, corsair raids, the defected Houses' elite warriors.

### Southeast Asia — No-Man's-Land · ~18%
Marine-monster-savaged and import-starved; a discordant-bloc patchwork.

## Africa, Oceania, South America
- **North Africa–Sahel — No-Man's-Land · ~18%** (mixed Bloc 14). **West Africa/Lagos — No-Man's-Land · ~19%** (coastal famine epicenter). **Central Africa — No-Man's-Land · ~18%** (nomad Bloc 4). **East & Southern Africa — No-Man's-Land · ~18%.** Africa's coasts are among the worst famine fronts on Earth.
- **Oceania & Pacific Is. — No-Man's-Land · ~19%** (corsair-raided, marine-savaged).
- **South America North — No-Man's-Land · ~17%** (discordant flotilla Bloc 8). **South America South — Embattled · ~17%** (a far-southern contested refuge).

## Travel & the mobility graph
Powered long-range transport is dead (EMP); oceanic shipping is permanently severed (marine monsters). Movement is on foot, horse, sail, salvaged pre-electronic vehicles, and — for the rare empowered flyer — the air. Travel between regions is a hazard in itself: the road *is* an adventure. Redoubts rebuild internal links first; the inter-regional network stays broken all year.

*All regions are starter-depth; each can be expanded into its own file with sites, local NPCs, and encounter tables keyed to its lethality number.*
