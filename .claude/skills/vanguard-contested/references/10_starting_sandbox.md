## THE SHAPE OF THE RUN
The campaign opens in **Manhattan at ~10:00**, runs the **corridor's fall** over the first ~90 days, and bends toward the **evacuation west** to the Interior North redoubt frontier. Geographically: Manhattan → across the Hudson into the Jersey corridor → west through Pennsylvania and the Alleghenies → a river crossing → the Interior North muster-line at **Marrow Creek**. The road *is* the campaign; each leg below is a region of its own.

Powered long-range transport is dead. Movement is on foot, on horse, on sail, on salvaged pre-electronic vehicles, and — for the rare empowered flyer — the air. Every leg west drops the lethality number; every leg west also tightens the Redoubt Authority's grip, which is its own kind of danger.

## A · DAY-ZERO MANHATTAN — *No-Man's-Land forming · ~18% · Tension floor 5 (opening scene 6)*
The first hours. The grid is gone, the sky is wrong, one in five people on the block just became something else, and the island has an afternoon before it goes fully dark. The factions here are the **collapse of standing institutions** (file 04, Day-Zero NYC) — not the named crosser blocs, which have not arrived. Power is precinct-by-precinct, building-by-building, block-by-block.

The crossings are everything: **bridges and tunnels jam instantly with dead vehicles**, and whoever holds the George Washington, the Lincoln, the Holland, or the tunnels controls who gets off the island. The medical centers become the most fought-over sites in the city.

**Keyed Manhattan sites** *(opening sandbox — the survivor is somewhere in this)*:

- **A besieged medical center** *(hospital · the EMP killed life support at the worst possible moment)* — Bellevue / the NYP and Mount Sinai campuses / the VA. Mass casualties from the pulse, no monitors, no ventilators, surgeons working by window-light. Everyone wants in; the staff and whoever's guarding the doors decide who lives. *The prize a medically-skilled survivor is pulled toward.*
- **A precinct station-house holding its block** *(precinct/armory · occupied, holding order)* — the most powerful armed institution on most blocks; runs a curfew, predates, or deserts depending on its captain. Armed, organized, and increasingly autonomous.
- **A high-rise turned fortified island** *(high-rise/tower · occupied by private security)* — helicopters won't fly, so the wealthy can't leave; their security and stockpiles wall off a tower. A redoubt-in-miniature with food and a generator behind armed men.
- **A crossing under contention** *(bridge/crossing · contested by two powers)* — the GW or a tunnel mouth, jammed with dead cars and the desperate on foot, with a precinct remnant and a street crew both claiming the toll. Control of it is control of the exodus.
- **A mutual-aid block that's still alive** *(church/mosque/synagogue or a bodega network · a desperate group inside)* — the real survival layer: a congregation, a building super, a union local feeding its people. The seed of a holdout enclave, and the one place a newcomer might be taken in — for a price or a pledge.

**Manhattan encounter, Day-Zero (d8, weight toward chaos):** 1 a manifesting stranger losing control of a power · 2 a crowd surging a crossing · 3 a precinct checkpoint conscripting the able · 4 a medical center's triage line breaking down · 5 a street crew seizing fuel/food · 6 a feral thing loose in a stairwell or a flooded subway · 7 a private-security cordon turning people away · 8 someone from the survivor's own life, in trouble, in the wrong place.

## B · THE MID-ATLANTIC CORRIDOR — *No-Man's-Land · ~18% · Tension floor 6*
The contested belt being given up: New York's outer boroughs, the Jersey sprawl, Philadelphia, Baltimore, the DC approaches. Governance has relocated inland; what's left is **holdout enclaves, scavenger crews, the desperate who stayed, and the Reaver raid-lines pressing down from New England**. This is the frontier's lethal edge and its richest adventure ground — the corridor is where most early characters die.

- **Holdout-borough enclaves** — a few blocks that organized and held, ringed by the dark. They trade, they conscript, they turn people away. (See file 14 for rolled examples.)
- **Dead hospitals and dark skyscrapers** — stripped or booby-trapped, full of what couldn't be carried and what crept in after.
- **Reaver raid-lines** — war-bands working south, named and peelable (file 13): **Yorl Ashwidow** (drags a captive refugee-mass, deals if it pays), **Drevel Coldmarrow** (a fast nomadic raiding column, no base to treat with), **Varn Saltbroke** (mass-dragging and openly hostile — the one you flee).
- **The evacuation routes themselves** — choked with the refugee column, preyed on at every chokepoint.

## C · THE ROAD WEST — five legs *(engine-rolled, file 08 site + complication)*
The spine of the campaign's middle. Each leg is a band, a defining site, and the thing wrong with it. Lethality drops as the survivor moves inland; the Authority's reach rises.

**Leg 1 — The Tower on the Corridor** · *No-Man's-Land · [site d20=9 / wrong d20=1]*
A **high-rise tower occupied by a faction** — a powered strongman or a fortified crew holding a vertical fief over a chokepoint of the route, taxing or pressing everyone who passes. The first hard gate out of the dead city. *Hook: pay, fight (above-tier, likely lethal), or find the way around through the dark below.*

**Leg 2 — The Camp That Turned** · *No-Man's-Land · [site d20=17 / wrong d20=5]*
A **refugee camp with a desperate group inside** — a swollen wayside camp on the evacuation route where the food ran out and the strong started taking. Sanctuary and predation in the same fence-line. *Hook: it's the only shelter for miles, and staying the night has a cost.*

**Leg 3 — The Stronghold That's Too Quiet** · *Embattled · [site d20=15 / wrong d20=20]*
A **faction stronghold where nothing seems wrong — which is itself the problem.** A walled holding that offers rest, trade, and a warm meal, and asks only a few questions. A press-gang, a cult, or a harvest-of-the-able behind a clean face. *Hook: the welcome is real until you try to leave.*

**Leg 4 — The Stripped Crossing** · *Embattled · [site d20=13 / wrong d20=9]*
A **river docks/harbor, already stripped** — the Allegheny or the upper Ohio crossing, the boats and fuel long gone, the warehouses picked clean, a few scavengers and a ferryman who charges in medicine. The water is the barrier; the swarm runs the rivers too. *Hook: no easy way across, and the wrong currency.*

**Leg 5 — The Water Plant Gate** · *Guarded · [site d20=5 / wrong d20=18]*
A **water-treatment plant that is a moral trap** — the first redoubt-controlled site, run by the Authority, that gives clean water and a registration line in the same building. Help, shelter, and the system's hook: register, be classed, and be conscripted. *Hook: the safe thing and the costly thing are the same door.* This is the threshold of Marrow Creek.

## D · MARROW CREEK — the Interior North muster-town · *Guarded · ~8% · Tension floor 4*
*[base-town name d6=6 · garrison extra tag d20=6 Zealots]*

A **reconstituted town under emergency authority**, the forward muster-camp facing the corridor — the safest the survivor will be all year, which is not very safe. The **Redoubt Authority** garrisons it; file 03's Interior North runs here. Patchwork power is returning, the ration economy is law, and a Compact enclave-town sits a few miles off. It is the home base, the place to heal lingering injuries, resupply (against the supply track, never for free), court factions, and take the Authority's missions.

**The garrison runs hard.** The rolled **Zealot** streak means Marrow Creek's command is *ideological about order* — the Marshal's people believe the line holds only through total registration, conscription, and discipline, and they enforce it like a creed. Refuge here is conditional and watched; the Exposure clock does not sleep just because the band is Guarded. (The autonomy-vs-conscription tension, file 04, is sharpest here.)

**Keyed Marrow Creek sites** *(its unsecured edges — danger is present even at home)*:

- **The Quarry Lair** *(site d20=19 monster lair / wrong d20=3 feral-infested)* — an old quarry or rail-cut on the town's edge where feral have denned; the garrison contains it but hasn't cleared it, and it presses on the perimeter. A standing local threat and a recurring muster job.
- **The Rigged Field Hospital** *(site d20=1 hospital / wrong d20=2 booby-trapped)* — a medical building on the line that was wired during an earlier withdrawal and never fully made safe; the town's medical need and a literal trap in the same walls. *A medically-skilled survivor will be sent in here.*
- **The Empty Depot** *(site d20=3 warehouse / wrong d20=9 already stripped)* — the supply warehouse, drawn down to the boards. The town's scarcity made concrete: the depot that should be full isn't, and the ration line knows it.

**Marrow Creek services & hooks:** relief-and-registration hub (classed and conscripted); a thin clinic (medicine is scarce — file 13 medical factions matter); a muster-camp taking the Authority's jobs; the Compact enclave-town nearby (Sael-of-the-Open-Hand's people — file 12) for alien capability and diplomacy; the rebuilt rail spur as the only fast way deeper inland.
