# 13 · FRONTIER FACTION DOSSIERS
*The four powers that touch the starting sandbox, expanded from file 04 into runnable dossiers: leadership, assets, **peelable sub-factions**, internal tensions, the levers that move the relationship track, and the triggers that advance or reverse each clock. File 04 is the world's faction list; this is how the frontier ones are actually run, week to week.*

> **Relationship track (file 01):** −3 war · −2 hostile · −1 friction · 0 neutral · +1 cooperative · +2 allied · +3 sworn. Factions **remember and react** — a faction wronged adapts and retaliates; a faction helped opens doors and asks for more. **Diplomacy is sub-faction** for divided blocs (Reavers); unitary blocs (Authority, Compact, Concord) treat as wholes.

---

## THE REDOUBT AUTHORITY — *Tier 4, Strong · the PC's likely home*
The emergency government of the interior line: military remnants, organized empowered, and the Compact alliance, holding the frontier and feeding the refugee flood. At the starting sandbox it **is** Marrow Creek (file 10).

- **Leadership:** the **Marshal of the Interior Line** (file 12) commands here; the Marrow Creek garrison carries a **Zealot** streak — its officers treat total registration and discipline as the one thing holding the line.
- **Assets:** hardened military remnants and armories; the **registration-and-conscription** apparatus; the ration economy; the rebuilt rail spur; the Compact alliance; forward muster-camps facing the corridor.
- **Sub-factions (peelable internally):**
  - *The Line* — regular defense units; pragmatic, exhausted, loyal to the wall.
  - *The Zealot order-faction* — the hard-liners who run registration like a creed; over-reach here is a live **Exposure** risk even inside the wire.
  - *The Quartermaster's office* — controls the stripped depot (file 10); rationing is power, and it's bled thin.
  - *The conscripted-empowered corps* — powered people classed and placed by the system; the seat of the **autonomy-vs-conscription** grievance.
- **Internal tension:** autonomy vs. duty; the *fairness* of the empowered-class system; Zealot discipline vs. pragmatist mercy. A PC can play these factions against each other.
- **Track levers:** **+** hold the wall, run muster jobs (file 14), bring in the empowered and refugees, surrender power-tells honestly. **−** desert, conceal a manifestation, cause collateral, defy a lawful order publicly.
- **Clock — Redoubt Recovery (8):** *advances* when sites are secured, power/water restored, the Compact deepened, the Tide repelled; *stalls/reverses* under famine, lost ground, atrocity that costs the Compact. Show it as visible capacity returning.

## THE INTERIOR COMPACT — *Bloc 3 · Tier 5, Strong · +2 allied · unitary*
The principled refuge-seekers anchoring the North American interior — **the reason the redoubt holds.** Mixed human-Compact settlements; alien capability on humanity's side. An enclave-town sits a few miles from Marrow Creek.

- **Leadership:** **Sael-of-the-Open-Hand** (Titan, file 12) — envoy and war-leader, patient, grieving a homeland the Unmaking took, *watching how humanity governs before committing the Compact's full weight.*
- **Assets:** above-human capability (deployed sparingly); the enclave-town; engineering and medicine beyond the human salvage economy; legitimacy that steadies the whole frontier.
- **Treat as a whole** (unitary bloc) — but Sael's *favor* is conditional and personal: it is granted to demonstrated justice and withdrawn from cruelty.
- **Internal tension:** how much to spend on a people who may yet prove faithless; grief vs. commitment; the Compact's own losses pressing them toward caution.
- **Track levers:** **+** govern justly (Sael's test — *"show me how you decide who eats"*), protect Compact people, keep faith under pressure. **−** cruelty, betrayal, letting the redoubt become the thing the Compact fled.
- **Clock interaction:** the Compact's confidence **models the Concord** — visible Compact trust pulls the Concord Swing toward commitment; visible Compact disillusion pushes it toward defection. Winning the Compact's heart is the strategic lever on the Concord's head.

## THE REAVERS — *Bloc 9 · Tier 3, Weak-but-fierce · −2/−3 · divided (sub-faction)*
Decentralized high-per-capita warbands out of New England, pressing south through the Mid-Atlantic. **No throne to treat with** — each war-chief is a faction. They are crossers who **believe they are also refugees owed land**, which makes them dealable in a way the Empire never is. *Diplomacy is one band at a time.*

- **Paramount-ish:** **Hesk Gravewater** (Warlord, file 12) holds the Connecticut approaches and the most standing — but cannot command the others, only out-weigh them.
- **The named war-bands** *(engine-rolled — the sub-faction roster of the Reaver Tide):*
  - **Yorl Ashwidow's band** *(tag: Refugee Mass · disposition: transactional)* — drags a captive/camp-follower mass behind it; slow, hungry, and therefore **dealable** — it raids to feed the mass, so food, safe passage, or ground can buy it off. The most peelable of the three. *Lever: trade, not battle.*
  - **Drevel Coldmarrow's band** *(tag: Nomadic · disposition: transactional)* — a fast raiding column with no fixed base: can't be besieged or starved out, can't hold ground or stockpile. Hits and vanishes. **Deal or evade; you can't pin it.** *Lever: a richer target elsewhere, or a toll for passage.*
  - **Varn Saltbroke's band** *(tag: Refugee Mass · disposition: hostile)* — mass-dragging and **openly hostile**, the cruelest of the three; takes captives, makes examples. **The one you flee or break, not bargain with** — though even Varn answers to hunger.
  - **The Knot-Sisters** (two Heavies, file 12) — raid-leaders **tired of dying for nothing**; the highest-value early peel. Turn them and a war-band leaves the Tide.
- **Internal tension:** chiefs compete for standing; Hesk's land-claim ideology vs. bands that just want to survive the winter; the tired (Knot-Sisters) vs. the zealous (Varn).
- **Track levers:** **+** offer a shared enemy, tribute, safer ground, an honor-debt repaid; treat with a band as people. **−** kill a chief (you make a martyr and harden the band), refuse parley, break a deal — Reavers remember.
- **Clock — the Reaver Tide (4):** *advances* when raids land unanswered and the corridor cedes ground; *reverses* when bands are peeled or bought off, when the Authority holds a line publicly, or when Hesk is given a reason to look elsewhere. The two most player-responsive clocks on the frontier are this and the Concord Swing — make their motion visible.

## THE ATLANTIC CONCORD — *Bloc 17 · Tier 5, Strong · +1 wavering toward 0 · unitary*
The advanced North-Atlantic people — **humanity's crown-jewel potential ally**, hedging toward neutral while humanity looks doomed. Their hesitation is *why the Mid-Atlantic falls.* **Winning them fully is a campaign-scale objective.**

- **Leadership:** the **First Voice of the Concord** (Crown, file 12) — precise, sorrowful, moved by demonstrated resolve and never by pleading.
- **Assets:** elite per-capita capability held in reserve; observers watching the frontier; the single most consequential commitment on the board. Barely in contact (comms dark) — they judge by what they can *see*.
- **Treat as a whole** — but the whole is *deciding*, and it decides on evidence.
- **Internal tension:** *"we have buried one world"* — the Concord weighs every commitment against the cost of betting on a people who may collapse anyway. Caution is not cowardice to them; it is grief made policy.
- **Track levers:** **+** demonstrated competence and resolve — battles won, ground held, just governance, the Compact's visible trust. **−** every collapse, atrocity, or broken line the observers witness.
- **Clock — the Concord Swing (6, two-way, starts 3/6 neutral):** toward **+** humanity proves it can hold → the Concord commits (a vast ally, a campaign win); toward **−** visible collapse → it defects. **The most consequential clock the PC can move** — and the slowest to earn. Tie its motion to things the PC actually did near a Concord witness.

## RUNNING THE FRONTIER
- **Advance clocks** at session end, every in-world week, and whenever the PC wastes time (file 04). Show only the perceivable ones — the Reaver Tide on your block is visible pressure; the Empire's consolidation is rumor.
- **Generate new local factions** as the corridor falls (file 04 procedure + file 14's pre-rolled examples). Prune the dead; grow the new; the map should look different by Day +90.
- **The frontier's spine:** the Authority feeds and conscripts you, the Compact tests and steadies you, the Reavers bleed you (and can be peeled), and the Concord watches everything you do and keeps score. Play one against another; that is the early game's real geometry.
