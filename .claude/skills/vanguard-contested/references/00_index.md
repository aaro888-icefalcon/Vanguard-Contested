# 00 · INDEX — Vanguard Contested

A playable RPG world built on a validated whole-world simulation of the Emergence. *Vanguard Contested* is the canon: the Pulse, the EMP, the monster transformation, and eighteen humanoid crosser nations fleeing the **Unmaking** all strike inside one week; the unified **Empire** leads a hostile coalition; humanity is beaten in the open but holds a defended **redoubt**. ~1.45B dead in year one; ~977M displaced. Organized defeat, with somewhere to stand.

**Play:** Mutants & Masterminds 3e, gritty simulation, **PL 8–9**. The campaign opens at **Day Zero in New York City** and runs **one in-world year** — the corridor's fall, the evacuation west, the redoubt forming. See `SKILL.md`.

## Provenance
Built from a multi-layer simulation: a branching casualty ensemble, an endogenized 18-bloc alignment game, a capability-ledger loop closure, and an EMP-corrected ~27-patch spatial model. The blocs, tiers, and capability shares are exact/stipulated canon; demographics are grounded; casualty and economic figures are scenario illustrations reported as ranges. Re-running the sim updates only the numbers in file 01 — the prose canon holds.

## Standing rules
History-book register, scale through consequence not gore; public roles by office, never named real individuals; nuclear forces signal but never detonate; no operational/weapons detail; every figure fictional.
