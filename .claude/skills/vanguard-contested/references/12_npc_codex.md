# 12 · NAMED NPC CODEX
*The named roster of file 05, expanded into runnable dossiers with stat lines, plus the engine-rolled seed cast for the starting sandbox. Each entry carries: tier/rung, what they want, how they bite, voice, peelability, and a stat reference (frames live in file 11). Crossers are peoples, not monsters — even enemies have reasons.*

> **Tier discipline.** Warlord+ figures are **above PL 8–9 caps** (file 01 gate) — they are statted to be fled, negotiated with, or out-thought, not dueled. Heavy/Siege figures get fair-fight or sub-boss blocks. **Voices are binding** — once a figure has spoken in a register, keep them there.

---

## HOW TO USE
- **Stat by reference.** Most entries point to a file 11 frame at their rung; unique figures get a custom line. Above-cap bosses are marked **[ABOVE PL]**.
- **Peelability.** Divided-bloc figures (Reavers, Pacific Houses) can be *turned* sub-faction; unitary-bloc figures (Empire, Concord, Compact) treat as wholes. Each dossier notes the lever.
- **The PC-tied slots.** Two rolled NPCs came up *connected to the PC's past/old life*. Since the PC is built at FIRST CHAT, **bind these at character creation** — attach them to the survivor's backstory, then run them as written. Defaults are given.

# THE NAMED ROSTER

## The Empire

### The Iron Regent — *Crown (~300×) · [ABOVE PL] · Tier 6*
The Empire's field commander in the West, charged with turning the heartland's weight toward the redoubts once consolidation completes (the **Empire Consolidates** clock; at 8/8 the Regent is unleashed). Cold, procedural; treats conquest as administration and sentiment as a rounding error.
- **Wants:** the line to advance on schedule. **Bites:** as a clock and a horizon, not an encounter — the Regent is the Year-Two threat made personal; meeting one is a death scene unless it's a parley the Regent permits.
- **Voice:** clipped, bureaucratic, never raised — *"The line moves on schedule. Sentiment is not a variable."*
- **Stat:** narrative boss far above file 11's Empire Officer; do not block for a duel. If forced, Crown-tier: Toughness 16+, Impervious 12+, area command effects, escort cohort always present.

### Vurn of the Seventh Spear — *Warlord · [ABOVE PL] · Empire vanguard*
The vanguard officer most likely to be the redoubt's first *named* Empire foe — the tip of the spear that tests the interior line. Ambitious, correct, hungry for the Regent's notice.
- **Wants:** a breakthrough to report. **Bites:** leads cohorts (file 11 Empire Vanguard Troopers) and brings an above-PL officer block; appears as the Reaver Tide and Empire probes mature.
- **Voice:** formal, impatient, contemptuous of disorder — *"Your people fight bravely. Bravery is what the unprepared bring instead of a plan."*
- **Stat:** **file 11 · Empire Vanguard Officer (PL 12, ABOVE PL).**

## The Reavers *(New England; sub-faction — file 13)*

### Hesk Gravewater — *Warlord · [ABOVE PL] · paramount-ish chief*
The closest thing the decentralized Reavers have to a paramount, holding the Connecticut approaches. Charismatic, capricious, and **genuinely believes the Reavers are refugees owed land** — which makes him negotiable in a way an Empire officer never is.
- **Wants:** ground for his people, and the standing that comes from taking it. **Bites:** commands multiple war-bands; a fair fight with Hesk is a losing one. **Peel:** he can be *dealt with* (a bargain, a shared enemy, a tribute) far more readily than beaten.
- **Voice:** warm then sudden, fond of bleak proverbs — *"Everyone's running from something, friend. We just stopped."*
- **Stat:** scale **file 11 · Reaver War-Chief up to Warlord (ABOVE PL)** — Toughness 12+, Regeneration, command of bands; flee or deal.

### The Knot-Sisters — *two Heavies, paired · PL 8 each · peelable*
Raid-leaders under the Reaver banner who are **tired of dying for nothing** — the canonical peel. Fight as a pair (Teamwork, flank); reachable with the right offer (a way out, a safer ground, a debt of honor).
- **Wants:** to stop spending their band on Hesk's standing. **Bites:** a coordinated two-Heavy fight is lethal to a lone Breaker; **Peel:** the highest-value early Reaver turn — win them and a war-band leaves the Tide.
- **Voice:** clipped, dry, finishing each other — *"We counted the dead last winter." / "We stopped counting this one."*
- **Stat:** **file 11 · Powered Human Heavy frame (PL 8)** ×2, melee/raider loadout, **Teamwork + Set-up** between them.

## The Redoubt & its allies

### The Marshal of the Interior Line — *unpowered (or Heavy) · runs Marrow Creek · Tier 4*
The human authority commanding the NA redoubt's defense and the conscription system — the PC's likely commander and the face of the **autonomy-vs-duty** tension. At Marrow Creek the garrison runs with a **Zealot** streak (file 10): the Marshal's people treat total registration and discipline as the only thing holding the line, and enforce it like doctrine.
- **Wants:** the line to hold; every able body classed and placed. **Bites:** conscripts the PC, rations against the supply track, and *watches* — overreach by zealous subordinates is a live Exposure risk even inside the wire.
- **Voice:** plain, tired, unyielding — *"I don't need you to like the order. I need you on the wall when it comes."*
- **Stat:** authority, not a brawler — Expertise: Tactics/Admin +10, Leadership, a garrison of file 11 Militia Squads and a few Heavies. (If a Heavy: standard PL 8 frame, unpowered build.)

### Sael-of-the-Open-Hand — *Titan · [ABOVE PL] · Interior Compact envoy · Tier 5 · +2 allied*
The Compact's war-leader and envoy — **the ally who makes the NA redoubt viable.** Patient, curious about humans, grieving a homeland lost to the Unmaking. Above PL, but on humanity's side; the relationship is the point, not a fight.
- **Wants:** to understand who humanity *is* before committing the Compact's full weight; mixed human-Compact settlements that work. **Bites:** withdraws favor (and the redoubt's viability) if humanity proves cruel or faithless — a moral mirror with strategic teeth.
- **Voice:** formal, gentle, asks more than answers — *"Show me how you decide who eats. Then I will know who you are."*
- **Stat:** benevolent boss-tier; never an enemy block. Capability on tap for allies; its *withdrawal* is the threat.

### First Voice of the Concord — *Crown · [ABOVE PL] · Tier 5 · +1 wavering*
Representative of the advanced Atlantic Concord — humanity's crown-jewel potential ally, hedging toward neutral while humanity looks doomed. **Winning the Concord fully (the Concord Swing clock) is the campaign's great diplomatic prize.**
- **Wants:** proof humanity can hold before spending Concord lives on it. **Bites:** every visible collapse near the PC swings the clock toward defection; the First Voice is moved by demonstrated resolve, not pleas.
- **Voice:** precise, sorrowful, maddeningly reasonable — *"We have buried one world. We are careful which graves we dig beside."*
- **Stat:** diplomatic boss; the encounter is persuasion and proof, resolved against the Concord Swing, not initiative.

## Humanity, in the wild

### The Breaker of Pittsburgh — *Siege · [ABOVE PL, sub-boss] · Tier 3 human power*
An empowered strongman who carved a fief from a corridor city — protector to his people, tyrant to everyone else. **The moral-gray human power the PC measures themselves against:** he does real good and real evil with the same hands, and he is one rung above the PC band.
- **Wants:** to keep his fief fed and unconquered, by any means. **Bites:** a Siege outmatches a Breaker PC; fighting him straight is likely fatal. **Lever:** he can be allied, undermined, out-maneuvered, or made into the lesser evil against a Reaver tide.
- **Voice:** blunt, proprietary, not cruel for its own sake — *"My people eat tonight. Yours can too, if you're mine by morning."*
- **Stat:** **Siege frame (PL 10–11, ABOVE PL):** STR/STA ~9–10, Toughness 11–12 w/ Impervious 9, a wall-breaking signature (Damage 11+), Dodge/Parry ~10. A sub-boss, not a peer.

### Doc — *Heavy · PL 7–8 · recurring ally*
A wandering healer-protector of the evacuation roads — regeneration and field medicine, a model of the quiet, decent use of power. The natural recurring ally and a living rebuke to the warlords. (Resonates with a medically-skilled PC — a peer, a mentor, or a rival in mercy.)
- **Wants:** to keep the road's wounded alive; nothing more, which is why everyone trusts him. **Bites:** never an enemy — but his presence anywhere draws the desperate, and protecting him is a recurring cost; losing him hurts the whole frontier.
- **Voice:** mild, wry, economical — *"I can't fix the world. I can fix you. Hold still."*
- **Stat:** **Powered Human Heavy frame (PL 7–8):** Regeneration 6–8, **Healing 8** (others; limited to trauma), Treatment/Medicine +12, Protection a few ranks, no real offense — he runs and mends, doesn't fight.

## Monster-lords

### The Drowning Crown — *Horror-tier, marine · [ABOVE PL] · campaign-scale*
A kaiju-class sapient that closed the North Atlantic shipping lanes — **the reason the sea off New England is death.** Not an encounter; a force of nature with a will, a clock, and a hunger.
- **Wants:** unknowable, oceanic, patient. **Bites:** as setting (severed sea, marine swarms driven inland, coastal death) and, rarely, as a moving catastrophe. **Interaction:** evade, evacuate, deny — never duel.
- **Stat:** **file 11 · Monster-Lord Frame** at the Horror ceiling; a campaign arc to even inconvenience.

# THE SEED CAST *(engine-rolled, file 08 NPC generator — the starting sandbox's living people)*

### Imani Voss — *criminal/fixer · the corridor's broker*
*[role 5 · wants 8 protect a secret · attitude 6 needs you · truth 4 already betrayed you]*
A fixer working the holdout-enclave black markets — the person who can get you across a crossing, into a stronghold, out of a press-gang. **Needs the PC** for something, **guards a secret**, and has **already sold someone out** to survive (maybe the PC's contact, before they ever met). Trust her work, never her loyalty. Sibling to Oziel. *Stat: unpowered, Deception/Persuasion/Streetwise high; backed by a few Militia Minions.*

### Dev Cho — *from the PC's old life · [BIND AT CREATION]*
*[role 20 someone from the PC's old life · wants 9 redemption · attitude 6 needs you · truth 5 empowered and hiding it]*
Someone the survivor knew before the Pulse, surfaced on the road, **seeking redemption** for an old failure and **hiding that they manifested too** — afraid of what it makes them, afraid the PC will see it. Bind the relationship at FIRST CHAT (a colleague, an ex, a friend they wronged or who wronged them). *Stat: **Powered Human Bruiser/Heavy frame**, power concealed (Stealth on the effect); the reveal is a scene, not a stat.*

### Mira Larkin — *mechanic/engineer · [BIND AT CREATION]*
*[role 4 · wants 11 escape · attitude 4 transactional · truth 12 connected to the PC's past]*
The one who keeps the salvaged pre-electronic vehicles running — priceless in a dead-grid world. **Wants out** (west, or further), deals **transactionally**, and is **tied to the PC's past** in a way she hasn't said. Engine of mobility for any crew; her price is always passage. *Stat: unpowered, Technology/Vehicles/Expertise: Mechanic +12, Equipment (a running vehicle — a relationship asset, file 14 relic-1).*

### Oziel Voss — *doctor/nurse · belonging, with someone to hide*
*[role 3 · wants 7 belonging · attitude 4 transactional · truth 7 protecting someone hidden]*
A medic working the camps and clinics, trading care for a place to belong, **hiding someone** behind the supply curtain (a manifesting child? a marked patient? a wanted person?). Imani's sibling — the soft hand to her hard one. A natural ally to a medical PC, and a moral hook: the hidden person is a liability the PC may be asked to share. *Stat: unpowered, Treatment/Medicine +10; a Medical-Lifeline faction (file 13/14) often forms around people like Oziel.*

### Cyrus Okafor — *refugee parent · afraid, and marked*
*[role 8 · wants 2 food/meds · attitude 2 afraid · truth 11 marked by an enemy]*
A parent on the evacuation road with kids and nothing, **desperate for food and medicine**, **afraid of everyone**, and **carrying a mark** — a faction or a war-band wants him, and proximity is dangerous. The human tide made personal: help him and inherit his hunters; pass him by and live with it. *Stat: unpowered non-combatant; his **mark** is a seeded Exposure/hunt clock (file 08 — the hunters arrive).*

### Nadia Mwangi — *empowered free agent · the curious crosser-sympathizer*
*[role 12 · wants 11 escape · attitude 5 curious · truth 10 a crosser sympathizer/agent]*
A manifested free agent, **curious** about the PC and **looking for a way out**, who **quietly sympathizes with (or works for) a crosser sub-faction** — she sees the crossers as fellow refugees, not enemies, and may be a back-channel to the Compact or a peelable Reaver band. Ambiguous: ally, double, or both. *Stat: **Powered Human Heavy frame (PL 8)**; roll/choose her power (file 08) — mobility or perception suits an agent.*
